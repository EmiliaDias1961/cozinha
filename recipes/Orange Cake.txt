400g oranges
120ml milk
80ml sunflower oil
150g sugar
250g all purpose flour
16g baking powder

Oven 180ºC
Cake pan lined with parchment paper

Cut the oranges into small pieces. If they have too much white rind fell free to peel it, but you have to keep the orange rind.
Place the oranges with the milk and oil in a food processor and blend untill smooth. Add eggs and sugar and process a few minutes until full combined.
Transfer the batter into a bowl and mix the flour and the baking powder.
Pour the batter in the cake pan and bake for at least 35 minutes. Insert a skewer to test the batter.
